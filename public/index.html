My spending

Every Apple Pay purchase, tracked automatically. Set a daily limit and get an alert when you go over.

On iPhone, add this to your Home Screen first so alerts work: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, and sign in from the new icon.

At least 10 characters. You need an invite code from the person who shared this app.

Your purchases are private to your account. Only you can see them, and you can delete your account and all its data any time in the app.

Connect your iPhone

One Shortcut sends each Apple Pay purchase here the moment you pay. This takes about 3 minutes.

  1. Copy your setup code. It links the Shortcut to your account. Keep it private.
    Loading…
  2. Add the Shortcut. When it asks, paste your setup code. Get the Shortcut Ask the person who shared this app for the Shortcut link.
  3. Make two automations in the Shortcuts app: tap Automation, then +.
    • Wallet: pick your cards and Run Immediately, then choose Log purchase. This catches tap to pay.
    • Notification: pick the Wallet app and Run Immediately, then choose Log purchase. This catches online Apple Pay.
  4. Pay with Apple Pay once. It shows up here within a few seconds.

How this is worked out
Purchases
Each purchase your phone sends after an Apple Pay payment, tap or online, saved privately to your account.
Duplicates
A tap and a Wallet notification for the same amount within ten minutes count once.
Categories
Matched from the merchant name first. If that isn't recognised, the category your phone picked is used. Anything else is Other.
Refunds
Counted as negative spending, so they lower the totals.
Left out
Declined payments, test entries with no amount, and anything not paid with Apple Pay.
