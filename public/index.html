Connect your iPhone

Two small Shortcuts send each Apple Pay purchase here the moment you pay. It takes about 5 minutes, once.

  1. Copy your setup code. It links your Shortcuts to your account. Keep it private.
    Loading…
  2. Add the tap-to-pay Shortcut. It catches payments when you tap your card or phone. Add “Log tap purchase” Ask the person who invited you for the link.
  3. Add the online-payment Shortcut. It catches Apple Pay payments made in apps and websites. Add “Log notification purchase” Ask the person who invited you for the link. For each one: tap Add Shortcut and paste your setup code when it asks. If it doesn’t ask, open the Shortcut and replace the words “Paste here” in the Text box with your code.
  4. Turn each one on. Open the Shortcut. At the top you’ll see a box that says “Automation is turned off”. Tap it, switch it on, and choose Run Immediately (not “Ask Before Running”). Do this for both Shortcuts.
  5. Make a small Apple Pay purchase. It should show up here within a few seconds.
    Waiting for your first purchase…
  6. For alerts, add this app to your Home Screen. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, open it from the new icon, sign in, then turn on alerts in Settings (the gear).

How this is worked out
Purchases
Each purchase your phone sends after an Apple Pay payment, tap or online, saved privately to your account.
Duplicates
A tap and a Wallet notification for the same amount within ten minutes count once.
Categories
Matched from the merchant name first (Uber Eats, DoorDash and similar are Food Delivery; convenience stores, candy and ice cream are Snacks). If that isn't recognised, the category your phone picked is used. Anything else is Other.
Refunds
Counted as negative spending, so they lower the totals.
Left out
Declined payments, test entries with no amount, and anything not paid with Apple Pay.
